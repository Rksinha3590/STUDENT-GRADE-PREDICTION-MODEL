Grade Prediction Module

Accepts input from users, such as current grades and attendance, and generates predicted grades for specific subjects or overall academic performance.

Early Warning System

It incorporates an early warning system that identifies students at risk of failing or struggling based on grade predictions, enabling timely intervention and support.

Feature Selection

The software can automatically or manually select the most relevant features and variables that contribute to predicting student grades, considering factors such as previous grades, attendance, study habits, and socioeconomic factors.

Reporting and Analytics

Generates comprehensive reports and analytics based on predicted grades and historical data, enabling educators and administrators to gain insights and identify areas for improvement.
